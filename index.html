Who We Are

About IEEE
Student Branch

The IEEE Student Branch at Karunya Institute of Science and Technology is a vibrant community of aspiring engineers and technologists dedicated to advancing innovation and excellence in electrical and electronics engineering.

As part of the world's largest technical professional organization, we provide our members with access to cutting-edge research, industry connections, and opportunities for professional development that shape the future of technology.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the premier student organization fostering technological innovation and professional excellence, creating a community of future leaders who will shape the advancement of electrical and electronics engineering for the benefit of humanity.

  • Foster innovation and creativity
  • Build industry-ready professionals
  • Advance technological literacy

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge, skills, and opportunities through technical workshops, industry collaborations, research initiatives, and professional networking events that bridge academia and industry.

  • Organize impactful technical events
  • Facilitate industry connections
  • Support research and publications
